This page is under fixing. I am reviewing this page. I am a research engineer at Samsung Electronics.

I received Bachelor's degree in Statistics and Software Technology & Entrepreneurship Program, and Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering both at Korea University, in 2023 and 2025, respectively.

.

My research goal is to develop a model that can predict future states accurately through Big Data Analysis and contribute to solving social problems in the world. To this end, I am deeply interested in methods aimed at representing and analyzing complex social phenomena using Statistical Machine Learning and Large Language Models with higher accuracy and efficiency (e.g. Multivariate Time-Series Forecasting & Anomaly Detection, Large Language Model-based Application, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation).
