Jaeseung Lee

Research Engineer, AI Center, DS Division, Samsung Electronics
jason2133@korea.ac.kr

I am a Research Engineer at AI Center, DS Division, Samsung Electronics. I develop large language model (LLM)-based intelligent agents for semiconductor manufacturing execution systems (MES), with a focus on automating engineering workflows and providing knowledge-aware decision support for manufacturing operations. Before joining Samsung Electronics, I gained industry experience through internships at Hyundai MOBIS and NAVER Cloud in 2025.

I received my Bachelor's degrees in Statistics and Software Technology & Entrepreneurship Program, as well as my Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering, from Korea University in 2023 and 2025, respectively.

My research goal is to advance AI-driven prognostics and health management (PHM) for intelligent manufacturing systems. To this end, I develop AI methods for fault diagnosis, anomaly detection, and PHM, with the aim of improving the reliability, safety, and efficiency of complex industrial systems. My research integrates machine learning, explainable AI, LLMs, and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) to provide trustworthy and human-centered decision support for next-generation autonomous manufacturing.

You can learn more about my professional background and research through the links below to my LinkedIn, GitHub, and Google Scholar profiles.
